Wordle solver

Assume the editor rarely reuses a word that has already been a Wordle answer, and that the ones that do come back are usually early ones. Makes already-used words less likely.

Assume most answers come from the curated 2022 list of answer words. Makes words outside this list less likely to be answers, and the ones that look answerable likelier than the rest.

Only guess words that could still be the answer. A word that cannot win is often the sharper probe, so this usually costs a little; f was fitted without the restriction, so its scores read slightly low under it.

What a guess is scored on: the total guesses it is expected to cost, or the bits it is expected to reveal right now.

The family of curves f(n) is fitted within. Only used by the expected-guesses mode.

Play against a word you choose. Leave it empty to have one drawn at random (subject to priors).

Changing any of these starts a fresh board.

              What the numbers are
              
      
              
        
              Words left is how many words would still be possible if you had
          played that guess and seen that pattern — the answer among them.
          Fewest first is the guess that would have told you the most about this
          particular answer.
              
        
              Bits is what the guess reveals on average over every answer it
          could have faced, not just this one. It does not depend on the pattern,
          so it ranks the words as openings rather than as hindsight.
              
        
              Chance is the prior's own probability that the guess is itself
          the answer, so it moves with the two prior switches. So does the bits
          column; the pattern match itself does not.
