Wordle solver

Assume the editor rarely reuses a word that has already been a Wordle answer. Makes already-used words less likely.

Assume most answers come from the curated 2022 list of answer words. Makes words outside this list less likely to be answers.

What a guess is scored on: the total guesses it is expected to cost, or the bits it is expected to reveal right now.

The family of curves f(n) is fitted within. Only used by the expected-guesses mode.

Play against a word you choose. Leave it empty to have one drawn at random (subject to priors).

Changing any of these starts a fresh board.
